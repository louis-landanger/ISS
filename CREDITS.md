# Credits and scientific notes

## Textures

Textures by **Solar System Scope**, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- Educational source: https://edu.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- Maps are 2K equirectangular textures used on 3D spheres. Saturn's ring alpha strip is remapped radially onto ring geometry. Rendered lighting, scale, rotation, and atmosphere effects are added by Orbit.
- Dock thumbnails are rendered from the same 3D models and textures.

## Facts

- [NASA Solar System Exploration](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/)
- [NASA Planetary Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/)
- Individual NASA planet pages are linked from the interface.

Values are rounded. Diameters use mean diameters except Mercury and Venus, whose nearly spherical figures are rounded to the usual published values. Rotation refers to a sidereal rotation rather than the sunrise-to-sunrise solar day. Orbits are circular and illustrative; the scene does not show current positions, true relative sizes, or true relative distances. Neptune's texture has enhanced blue coloration.
